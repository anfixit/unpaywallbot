# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Project spaces should be professional, welcoming, and focused on constructive engineering work.

Expected behavior includes:

- treating other participants with respect
- discussing technical decisions rather than attacking people
- giving actionable feedback with evidence
- respecting privacy, copyright, and security boundaries
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them
- keeping issues and pull requests relevant to the project

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- publishing private information or credentials
- encouraging unauthorized access or harmful testing
- repeated disruption, spam, or bad-faith argument
- sexualized language or imagery
- retaliation against vulnerability reporters or reviewers

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community.

Reports should be sent privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not disclose sensitive personal or security information in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented when practical, and focused on protecting participants and the project.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where a participant represents the project.
